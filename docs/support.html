Killer Pool Social — Support

Help, contact, and common questions.

Contact

For account issues, bugs, privacy requests, or App Review questions, email us at killerpoolsocial@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few business days.

What the app does

Killer Pool Social includes Timer Pool (timed runs and leaderboards), multiplayer Killer Pool rooms, social feed posts, friends, and head-to-head records. Sign in or continue as a guest to try core features.

Common issues

  • Sign-in / password — Use the same username and password you registered with. Passwords are case-sensitive.
  • Joining a room — Enter the room code from your host. Rooms in progress may not accept new players.
  • Timer runs not saving — Sign in before finishing a run. Very short runs may not qualify for the leaderboard.
  • Photos / camera — Allow Camera and Photos access in iOS Settings if you use profile or feed images.
  • Notifications — Enable notifications in the app and in system Settings if you want friend and activity alerts.
